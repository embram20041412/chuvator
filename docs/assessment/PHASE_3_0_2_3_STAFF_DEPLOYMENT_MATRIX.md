### 3. EMERGENCY RESPONSE & CLINICAL CHAIN OF COMMAND

If an acute medical emergency occurs during combine testing:
1. **Immediate Halt Authority:** Staff 02 (Physiotherapist) and Staff 01 (Lead Sports Scientist) possess unilateral authority to halt an individual athlete's test or the entire combine.
2. **On-Pitch First Aid Protocol:**
   - Staff 02 responds immediately with trauma kit and AED.
   - Staff 01 assumes operational communication, directs adjacent squads away from the incident area, and coordinates emergency vehicle egress.
   - Station testing at non-affected stations continues unless Staff 01 declares a combine-wide pause.
3. **Heat Illness Protocol:** If an athlete demonstrates signs of exertional heat distress (confusion, nausea, ataxia):
   - Immediate relocation to the air-conditioned / shaded Medical Pavilion.
   - Application of cold water immersion / ice towels to neck, axillae, and groin.
   - Vital signs logged every 3 minutes. Emergency medical services (118 / 112) dispatched if symptoms do not improve within 10 minutes.
