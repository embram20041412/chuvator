### PROTOCOL 1: FTB-UNI-PHY-01 — AKSELERASI

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-01` — Akselerasi
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Initial sprint acceleration capacity from a static start.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The rate of change in linear velocity over the initial positive horizontal force production phase ($0$ to $10\text{ m}$), characterized by forward trunk lean ($45^\circ$), triple extension of the hip, knee, and ankle, long ground contact times ($>0.15\text{ s}$), and high propulsive net horizontal impulses (Buchheit et al., 2014).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** $10\text{m}$ Stationary Start Linear Sprint, measured as the initial split of the integrated $30\text{m}$ combine sprint.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** In elite football, $>85\%$ of all match sprints are shorter than $15\text{ m}$. Initial $10\text{m}$ acceleration separates professional from amateur players and determines success in winning loose balls and penetrating defensive lines (Altmann et al., 2019).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Dual-beam wireless photocell timing gates (e.g., Microgate Witty, Smartspeed, or Brower). Single-beam gates are banned due to arm-swing error.
   - Start line marker cone placed $0.50\text{ m}$ behind the first gate.
   - Measuring tape ($50\text{m}$, steel or fiberglass).
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Photocell alignment verified using laser pointer. Timing gate height set to $0.80\text{ m}$ for athletes $<160\text{ cm}$ and $0.90\text{ m}$ for athletes $\ge 160\text{ cm}$. Receiver sensitivity calibrated to ambient sunlight.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Straight, dry, flat artificial turf or natural grass pitch. Start line at $0.0\text{ m}$; first timing gate at $0.50\text{ m}$ in front of the athlete's front foot; split gate at $10.0\text{ m}$; run-through zone of at least $15\text{ m}$ past the finish line.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up completed. Specific sprint potentiation: 2 progressive build-ups over $15\text{ m}$ at $80\%$ and $90\%$ effort. Two-minute passive rest before trial 1.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di belakang garis start (50 cm dari sensor). Gunakan stance split-stance (satu kaki di depan). Jangan goyang atau memantul sebelum mulai. Mulai saat Anda siap setelah aba-aba 'Siap... LARI!'. Lari secepat mungkin menembus gerbang 10 meter tanpa melambat!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 valid trials. Minimum rest interval between trials: **3 minutes** of passive/active walking recovery (work-to-rest ratio $\ge 1:90$).
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Time elapsed in seconds, recorded to **two decimal places** (e.g., `1.74 s`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Stationary split start without backward step, false start, or pre-motion. Unbroken sprint through $10\text{m}$.
    - *Invalid:* Rocking or false start; swinging hand triggering gate prior to torso passage; slipping or stumble. If invalid, allow 1 re-test after 3 minutes.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Electronic capture via console sync. Field scribe notes split time on paper card. The fastest (lowest) time of the 2 valid trials is retained as the raw score.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Absolute contraindication: acute hamstring, groin, or quadriceps strain within prior 21 days. Ensure run-through deceleration zone is clear of walls, fences, or personnel.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Valid from T1 (optional submax) to T4 (Performance). For T1, focus is motor literacy; electronic gate testing is standard from T2 (U11) upwards.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Crucial for wingers, strikers, fullbacks, and center-backs.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Buchheit, M., Samozino, P., Glynn, J. A., Michael, B. S., Al Haddad, H., Mendez-Villanueva, A., & Morin, J. B. (2014). Mechanical determinants of acceleration and maximal sprinting speed in young soccer players. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 32(20), 1906–1913. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2014.965191
    - Altmann, S., Ringhof, S., Neumann, R., Woll, A., & Rumpf, M. C. (2019). Validity and reliability of methods to assess speed characteristics in football: A systematic review. *Sports Medicine*, 49(6), 919–951. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-019-01086-6
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Electronic photocell timing over $10\text{m}$ exhibits excellent test-retest reliability ($\text{CV} \le 1.8\%$, $\text{ICC} > 0.90$). Limitation: Starting position ($0.50\text{ m}$ setback vs pressure plate trigger) affects initial time capture by ~0.10s.

### PROTOCOL 2: FTB-UNI-PHY-02 — KELINCAHAN

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-02` — Kelincahan
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** True reactive agility (perceptual-cognitive decision-making integrated with rapid physical change of direction).
3. **Scientific Definition:** A rapid whole-body movement with change of velocity or direction in response to an unpredictable external visual or auditory stimulus (Sheppard & Young, 2006). Distinct from Change of Direction Speed (CODS), which is pre-planned.
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Y-Shaped Reactive Agility Test (RAT) utilizing a high-intensity wireless light-pod trigger or 2D reactive timing system (e.g., Smartspeed Reactive or Fitlight).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Football matches require reactive cuts responding to ball trajectory, opponent cuts, and teammate movement. Pre-planned tests fail to assess the perceptual-cognitive component, which explains $>40\%$ of variance in elite agility performance (Paul et al., 2016).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 3 pairs of photocell timing gates (1 start/finish gate, 2 directional split gates positioned $45^\circ$ left and right at $5\text{ m}$).
   - 1 wireless reactive light-pod sensor mounted on a center tripod at $1.2\text{ m}$ height, $6\text{ m}$ from start.
   - Non-slip testing cones.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Light pod trigger latency verified to $<5\text{ ms}$. Timing gates aligned and leveled.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** $10\text{ m} \times 10\text{ m}$ grid on dry turf. Athlete starts at $0.0\text{ m}$. Sprint forward $4\text{ m}$. Light illuminates green on Left pod or red on Right pod at the $4\text{ m}$ mark. Athlete must process color, execute cutting maneuver at $45^\circ$, and sprint through the corresponding gate at $7\text{ m}$.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up with dynamic multi-directional cuts and visual-stimulus reaction drills (mirror drills).
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Sprint lurus ke arah lampu. Saat melintasi garis 4 meter, perhatikan lampu tengah: jika HIJAU, potong tajam ke KIRI menembus gerbang kiri; jika MERAH, potong tajam ke KANAN menembus gerbang kanan. Lakukan secepat mungkin tanpa ragu!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 4 total trials (2 left, 2 right, presented in randomized, counterbalanced sequence unknown to athlete). Rest interval: **3 minutes** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Total movement time in seconds, recorded to **two decimal places** (e.g., `2.38 s`). Average and best times recorded.
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Cut triggered strictly after visual illumination; athlete completes correct directional gate without touching cones.
    - *Invalid:* Guessing or cutting prior to light stimulus; missing gate; slipping. Invalid trial repeated after 3 minutes.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Console records total time and directional choice. Scribe notes trial number, direction, and time.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** High eccentric cutting forces. Contraindicated for athletes returning from ACL reconstruction $<9$ months post-op or acute ankle sprains.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Recommended for T3 (U14–U16) and T4 (Performance). Not recommended for T1 (Foundation), who lack mature visuo-motor processing.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal, highly predictive for attacking midfielders, wingers, and fullbacks.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Sheppard, J. M., & Young, W. B. (2006). Agility literature review: Classifications, training and testing. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 24(9), 919–932. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640410500457109
    - Paul, D. J., Gabbett, T. J., & Nassis, G. P. (2016). Agility in team sports: Testing, training and factors affecting performance. *Sports Medicine*, 46(3), 421–442. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-015-0428-2
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **REQUIRES SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION**. While the construct validity is superior to pre-planned tests, field reliability depends heavily on the visual stimulus technology. Standardized video-projection of human opponents is optimal in laboratory settings but commercially impractical in field combines. Light-pod proxy is acceptable provided stimulus timing is randomized.

### PROTOCOL 3: FTB-UNI-PHY-03 — KESEIMBANGAN

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-03` — Keseimbangan
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Dynamic balance, single-leg neuromuscular postural control, and lower extremity reach symmetry.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability to maintain a base of support on a single limb while performing maximal reach movements with the contralateral limb across three standardized directions without loss of balance (Plisky et al., 2006).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Y-Balance Test Lower Quarter (YBT-LQ).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** In football, all technical ball-striking, tackling, and cutting actions occur on a single leg. An anterior reach asymmetry $>4.0\text{ cm}$ or a composite score $<94\%$ is prospectively associated with a $3.5\times$ increased risk of non-contact lower extremity injury in soccer players (Plisky et al., 2006; Butler et al., 2013).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Standardized Y-Balance Test Kit (Move2Perform) consisting of a stance platform and 3 reach pipes marked in $0.5\text{ cm}$ increments oriented at Anterior ($0^\circ$), Posteromedial ($135^\circ$), and Posterolateral ($135^\circ$).
   - Cloth measuring tape for true leg length measurement (ASIS to medial malleolus).
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Ensure reach pipes slide smoothly without excessive friction or binding. Stance plate non-slip surface intact.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat, level indoor floor or firm artificial turf. Clear radius of $2.5\text{ m}$ around apparatus.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Performed barefoot or in athletic socks. Leg length measured in supine position prior to test. 6 practice reach trials per limb across all directions.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri dengan satu kaki di tengah platform (ibu jari kaki di garis batas). Pertahankan kedua tangan di pinggang. Dorong indikator merah sejauh mungkin dengan kaki lainnya secara perlahan dan kembali ke posisi tegak tanpa menyentuh lantai dengan kaki yang menjangkau."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 3 valid trials per direction (Anterior, Posteromedial, Posterolateral) per limb (Right and Left). Total: 18 trials. Rest: 15–20 seconds between reaches.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - Reach distance in centimeters to nearest $0.5\text{ cm}$.
    - Composite Score ($\%$):
      $$\text{Composite Score} = \left(\frac{\text{Ant} + \text{PM} + \text{PL}}{3 \times \text{Limb Length}}\right) \times 100$$
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Hands remain on hips; heel of stance foot remains flat on platform; reach foot pushes reach-indicator smoothly without kicking; balance recovered to standing position.
    - *Invalid:* Hands come off hips; stance foot lifts, shifts, or slides off platform; reach foot rests weight on slider or touches floor for support; athlete kicks slider. Invalid trial repeated immediately.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Evaluator reads reach distance at edge of slider plate. Record best reach of 3 valid attempts for each direction and calculate limb asymmetry ($|\text{Right} - \text{Left}|$).
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Safe, low injury risk. Contraindicated in athletes with acute ligamentous ankle instability or active concussion.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Highly recommended across all tiers: T1 (Foundation motor control), T2, T3 (crucial during PHV growth spurt where motor clumsiness occurs), and T4.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Extremely important for goalkeepers, fullbacks, and wingers.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Plisky, P. J., Rauh, M. J., Kaminski, T. W., & Underwood, F. B. (2006). Star Excursion Balance Test as a predictor of lower extremity injury in high school basketball players. *Journal of Orthopaedic & Sports Physical Therapy*, 36(12), 911–919. https://doi.org/10.2519/jospt.2006.2244
    - Butler, R. J., Lehr, M. E., Fink, M. L., Kiesel, K. B., & Plisky, P. J. (2013). Dynamic balance performance and noncontact lower extremity injury in college football players. *Sports Health*, 5(5), 417–422. https://doi.org/10.1177/1941738113498004
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. YBT-LQ has excellent inter-rater and intra-rater reliability ($\text{ICC} > 0.88$). Limitation: Highly dependent on true anatomical limb length measurement accuracy.

### PROTOCOL 4: FTB-UNI-PHY-04 — MOBILITAS

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-04` — Mobilitas
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Closed-chain weight-bearing ankle dorsiflexion mobility.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The maximum degree of closed-kinetic-chain ankle dorsiflexion achieved before compensatory heel lift or excessive pronation, reflecting talocrural joint arthrokinematics and soleus-gastrocnemius flexibility (Bennell et al., 1998).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Weight-Bearing Lunge Test (WBLT / Knee-to-Wall Test), utilizing a digital inclinometer (tibial angle) and linear distance tape.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Restricted weight-bearing ankle dorsiflexion ($<35^\circ$ or $<10\text{ cm}$ from wall) is a primary risk factor for patellar tendinopathy (jumper's knee), ACL injury mechanics (increased dynamic knee valgus), and recurrent lateral ankle sprains in footballers (Powden et al., 2019).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Digital inclinometer (or smartphone with validated clinometer app calibrated to $0.1^\circ$).
   - Millimeter tape measure secured perpendicularly along the floor against a solid wall.
   - Vertical plumb line or straight wall marker.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Digital inclinometer zero-referenced to a vertical wall and flat floor before every session.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat indoor floor directly adjacent to a solid vertical wall.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Performed barefoot. Light ankle mobilization (5 ankle circles, 5 calf raises).
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Posisikan kaki tegak lurus dengan dinding. Tekuk lutut maju ke depan hingga menyentuh garis penanda di dinding. Pastikan tumit tetap menempel kuat di lantai tanpa terangkat sedikit pun. Mundurkan kaki perlahan sampai menemukan jarak terjauh di mana lutut masih bisa menyentuh dinding tanpa tumit terangkat."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 3 valid trials per limb (Right and Left). Rest: 15 seconds between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - Primary: Maximum tibial angle to vertical in **degrees ($^\circ$)** to nearest $0.5^\circ$.
    - Secondary: Toe-to-wall distance in **centimeters** to nearest $0.1\text{ cm}$.
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Knee contacts center line of wall without lateral/medial deviation; heel remains flat on floor verified by evaluator's hand or paper strip under heel; contralateral foot remains comfortable.
    - *Invalid:* Heel rises from floor (evaluator can slide paper under heel); knee deviates inward into valgus collapse; foot rotates out. Invalid trial repeated.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Scribe records tibial angle ($^\circ$) and distance ($\text{cm}$) for right and left ankles. Calculate asymmetry ($|\text{Right} - \text{Left}|$).
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Contraindicated in acute ankle syndesmosis (high ankle sprain) injury or anterior ankle impingement with pain.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** All tiers: T1 to T4. Critical in T3 during rapid tibial longitudinal bone growth.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Bennell, K. L., Talbot, R. C., Wajswelner, H., Techovanich, W., Kelly, D. H., & Hall, A. J. (1998). Intra-rater and inter-rater reliability of a weight-bearing lunge measure of ankle dorsiflexion. *Australian Journal of Physiotherapy*, 44(3), 175–180. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0004-9514(14)60377-9
    - Powden, C. J., Hoch, J. M., & Hoch, M. C. (2019). Reliability and minimal detectable change of the weight-bearing lunge test: A systematic review. *Manual Therapy*, 20(4), 524–532. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.math.2015.01.004
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Exceptionally high inter-rater reliability ($\text{ICC} = 0.97–0.99$). The tibial angle measure is preferred over distance-to-wall because it is anthropometrically independent of foot length.

### PROTOCOL 5: FTB-UNI-PHY-06 — KECEPATAN MAKSIMAL

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-06` — Kecepatan Maksimal
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Maximum linear flying sprint velocity ($v_{\max}$).
3. **Scientific Definition:** The maximum speed attained in linear locomotion, characterized by upright trunk posture, vertical ground reaction force production, minimal ground contact times ($<0.10\text{ s}$), and high vertical spring-mass stiffness (Morin et al., 2012).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** $30\text{m}$ Linear Sprint with Flying $10\text{m}$ Split ($20\text{ m}$ to $30\text{ m}$).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Modern elite match play demands frequent high-speed running ($>19.8\text{ km/h}$) and sprinting ($>25.2\text{ km/h}$). Maximum sprint speed creates an "anaerobic speed reserve," allowing players to repeat submaximal efforts with lower relative physiological strain (Buchheit et al., 2014; Haugen et al., 2014).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 3 sets of dual-beam photocell timing gates positioned at $0.0\text{ m}$ (start), $10.0\text{ m}$ (acceleration split), $20.0\text{ m}$ (entry gate for flying sprint), and $30.0\text{ m}$ (finish gate).
   - Cones marking run-through and safety deceleration zone ($20\text{ m}$ past finish).
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Leveling lasers, receiver height set to $0.90\text{ m}$ (hip/iliac crest height).
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Straight, flat $50\text{ m}$ stretch on turf (30m sprint corridor + 20m runoff).
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Integrated into the standard combine sprint station following full RAMP warm-up.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Mulai dari posisi diam di belakang garis start. Lari dengan kecepatan penuh tanpa melambat sedikit pun sampai melewati kerucut penanda 30 meter. Pertahankan tubuh tegak dan ayunan langkah maksimal di 10 meter terakhir!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 valid trials. Minimum rest interval between trials: **4 to 5 minutes** (complete phosphocreatine resynthesis and CNS recovery).
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - Flying $10\text{m}$ split time ($20\text{m}$ to $30\text{m}$) in seconds to **two decimal places** (e.g., `1.08 s`).
    - Derived maximum velocity:
      $$v_{\max} = \frac{10.0}{\text{Split Time}} \times 3.6\text{ (km/h)}$$
      (e.g., $10.0 / 1.08 \times 3.6 = 33.33\text{ km/h}$).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Smooth acceleration, maximum effort sustained through $30\text{m}$ gate without premature deceleration or diving.
    - *Invalid:* Decelerating prior to $30\text{m}$ gate; tripping or false start.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Console records 0–10m, 10–20m, and 20–30m splits. Lowest 20–30m split time is recorded.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Hamstring strain risk is highest during the terminal swing phase of maximum velocity sprinting. Do not conduct in cold conditions ($<15^\circ\text{C}$) without active re-warm-up.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Mandatory for T2 (U11–U13), T3 (U14–U16), and T4 (Senior). Prohibited for T1 (Foundation), where sustained maximal sprinting lacks technical stability.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Highest tactical priority for Wingers (WFR/WFL), Fullbacks (LB/RB), Wingbacks (WB), and Strikers (ST).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Haugen, T. A., Tønnessen, E., Hisdal, J., & Seiler, S. (2014). The role and development of sprinting speed in soccer. *International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance*, 9(3), 432–441. https://doi.org/10.1123/ijspp.2013-0121
    - Morin, J. B., Bourdin, M., Edouard, P., Peyrot, N., Samozino, P., & Lacour, J. R. (2012). Mechanical determinants of 100-m sprint running performance. *European Journal of Applied Physiology*, 112(11), 3921–3930. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00421-012-2379-8
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Split timing over 20–30m has high reliability ($\text{CV} \le 1.5\%$). Limitation: In U12–U13 players, true peak velocity is often reached around 20–25m, whereas in elite seniors it occurs at 35–40m. A 30m corridor is the optimal standardized compromise.

### PROTOCOL 6: FTB-UNI-PHY-07 — REAKSI

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-07` — Reaksi
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Simple and choice visual reaction time.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The time interval between the presentation of an unanticipated visual sensory stimulus and the initiation of the motor response (Schmidt & Lee, 2011).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Multi-Choice Visual Reaction Time Test using a wireless light-pod array (e.g., Blazepod or Fitlight) in a standardized upper/lower extremity setup.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Football is an unpredictable open-skill sport requiring rapid perceptual processing of visual cues (opponent movement, ball deflection, goalkeeper anticipation).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 4 wireless LED target pods mounted on tripods at waist height ($0.90\text{ m}$) arranged in a semi-circle with $0.80\text{ m}$ radius from the central athlete stance.
   - Master controller tablet with millisecond timing resolution.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Optical touch sensors calibrated to light hand-tap ($<200\text{ g}$ force). Randomization interval program set to $1.5$ to $3.5\text{ s}$ delay.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Covered, shade-protected indoor area to eliminate sunlight interference on LED pods.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** 2 practice sequences of 5 taps to familiarize with pod sensitivity.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di tengah dalam posisi atletis siap bergerak. Begitu salah satu pod menyala, tepuk pod tersebut secepat mungkin menggunakan tangan terdekat. Segera kembali ke posisi tengah setelah menepuk."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 testing blocks of 15 random visual stimuli. Rest interval: 60 seconds between blocks.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Mean reaction time in **milliseconds (ms)** (e.g., `385 ms`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* 15 completed responses; athlete maintains athletic stance.
    - *Invalid:* Premature anticipatory tapping before illumination; false hits ($>2$ missed pods).
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Direct export from controller app. System records average response time across the 30 total stimuli.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Minimal physical injury risk.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** T2, T3, and T4. Optional for T1.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Crucial for Goalkeepers (`GK`), Center-Backs, and Central Midfielders.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Schmidt, R. A., & Lee, T. D. (2011). *Motor control and learning: A behavioral emphasis* (5th ed.). Human Kinetics.
    - Vestberg, T., Gustafson, R., Maurex, L., Ingvar, M., & Petrovic, P. (2012). Executive functions predict the success of top-soccer players. *PLoS ONE*, 7(4), e34731. https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0034731
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **REQUIRES SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION**. Generic visual reaction time tests using light pods have questionable transfer to sport-specific football anticipation, which relies on reading opponent hip posture and ball kinematics rather than abstract LED flashes. Validated as a general neurological processing speed baseline only.

### PROTOCOL 7: FTB-UNI-PHY-08 — LOMPATAN

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-08` — Lompatan
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Maximum vertical jump height and stretch-shortening cycle (SSC) performance.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The maximum vertical center-of-mass displacement achieved during a vertical countermovement jump without arm swing, utilizing the stretch-shortening cycle of the lower extremity extensors (Markovic et al., 2004).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Countermovement Jump (CMJ) with Hands on Hips on a calibrated contact mat or optical jump system (e.g., Optojump Next, Just Jump Mat, or Force Plates).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Vertical jumping is directly linked to aerial duels (defensive clearances by center-backs, offensive headers by strikers, and goalkeeping high-ball catches) and serves as the gold-standard neuromuscular fatigue and power indicator in football (Castagna et al., 2013).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Optical infrared jump system (Optojump) or electronic contact mat.
   - Flat, non-slip testing surface.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Contact mat zeroed. If using Optojump, bars leveled and parallel at $1.0\text{ m}$ spacing.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat $2\text{ m} \times 2\text{ m}$ testing zone.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up with dynamic jumping progression (3 ankle hops, 2 submaximal CMJs at $70\%$ and $85\%$).
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri tegak di tengah matras dengan kedua tangan di pinggang. Turunkan badan dengan menekuk lutut (countermovement) lalu melompat setinggi-tingginya secepat mungkin! Pertahankan tangan tetap di pinggang dan mendarat dengan kedua kaki di posisi yang sama tanpa menekuk lutut berlebihan di udara."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 3 valid trials. Rest interval: **60 seconds** between jumps.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Jump height in centimeters to **one decimal place** (e.g., `38.4 cm`), calculated from flight time ($t_f$):
    $$h = \frac{g \times t_f^2}{8} \times 100$$
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Hands remain firmly fixed on iliac crests throughout; no tucking of knees or dorsiflexion of feet during flight; landing within original footprint.
    - *Invalid:* Hands release from hips (arm swing artifact adds 10–15% height); knees bend in flight to delay landing ("tuck jump"); athlete lands outside contact mat.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Console records flight time ($t_f$) and calculated jump height. The highest jump of the 3 valid attempts is recorded.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Contraindicated in patellar tendinopathy flare-ups or acute knee pain. Ensure ceiling height $\ge 3.5\text{ m}$.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Mandatory for T2, T3, and T4. In T1 (U8–U10), use Sargent jump with chalk on wall to teach jump landing mechanics safely.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Paramount for Goalkeepers (`GK`), Center-Backs (`CB`), and Strikers (`ST`).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Markovic, G., Dizdar, D., Jukic, I., & Cardinale, M. (2004). Reliability and factorial validity of squat and countermovement jump tests. *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 18(3), 551–555. https://doi.org/10.1519/1533-4287(2004)18<551:RAFVOS>2.0.CO;2
    - Castagna, C., Ganzetti, M., Ditroilo, M., Giovannelli, M., Rocchetti, A., & Manzi, V. (2013). Concurrent validity of vertical jump tests in young soccer players. *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 27(1), 99–106. https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e3182510372
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. CMJ with hands on hips has near-perfect test-retest reliability ($\text{ICC} > 0.94$, $\text{CV} < 3.0\%$). Flight-time equations assume takeoff and landing center-of-mass positions are identical; proper landing form enforcement is mandatory.

### PROTOCOL 8: FTB-UNI-PHY-09 — PERUBAHAN ARAH

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-09` — Perubahan Arah
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Pre-planned change of direction speed (CODS) and Change of Direction Deficit (CODD).
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability to decelerate, turn through a predetermined angle ($180^\circ$), and re-accelerate linearly, independently of cognitive or perceptual stimuli (Nimphius et al., 2016).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** 505 Change of Direction Speed Test (505 Agility Test), evaluating both dominant and non-dominant turning limbs.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Unlike linear sprinting, $180^\circ$ turns evaluate eccentric braking strength, lateral hip stability, and propulsive re-acceleration. By calculating the **COD Deficit** ($\text{505 Time} - 10\text{m Linear Sprint Split}$), coaches isolate true turning capability from linear sprint speed bias (Nimphius et al., 2016).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 1 pair of dual-beam photocell timing gates.
   - Non-slip marker cones.
   - Chalk or high-visibility tape for the turning line.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Gate height set to $0.80\text{ m}$–$0.90\text{ m}$. Receiver aligned across a $1.5\text{ m}$ lane width.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** A $15\text{ m}$ straight corridor on dry pitch.
   - Start line at $0.0\text{ m}$.
   - Timing gate set at $10.0\text{ m}$.
   - Turning line marked at $15.0\text{ m}$ ($5\text{ m}$ past the timing gate).
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up with specific $180^\circ$ plant-and-cut drills at submaximal speed.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Mulai dari posisi diam di garis 0m. Lari sprint menembus gerbang 10 meter menuju garis putar di 15 meter. Letakkan satu kaki persis di atas atau melewati garis penanda 15 meter, putar balik 180 derajat, dan sprint secepatnya kembali menembus gerbang sensor!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 4 total trials: 2 turning off the Right limb, 2 turning off the Left limb. Rest interval: **3 minutes** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - Raw 505 time in seconds to **two decimal places** (e.g., `2.24 s`).
    - COD Deficit calculation:
      $$\text{COD Deficit (s)} = \text{505 Time} - (10\text{m Linear Split Time})$$
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Foot completely reaches or crosses the $15\text{ m}$ line; full $180^\circ$ turn without slipping; clear sprint back through timing gate.
    - *Invalid:* Athlete turns short of the $15\text{ m}$ line; slips or touches floor with hands; stumbles. Invalid trial re-tested after full rest.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Record best time for Right and Left turns. Compute turning limb asymmetry ($|\text{Right} - \text{Left}|$).
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** High knee adduction and internal rotation moments during plant. Surface must be completely dry.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Mandatory for T2 (U11–U13), T3, and T4.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Paramount for Fullbacks, Wingers, and Strikers.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Nimphius, S., Callaghan, S. J., Spiteri, T., & Lockie, R. G. (2016). Change of direction deficit: A more isolated measure of change of direction performance than total run time. *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 30(11), 3024–3032. https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000001421
    - Paul, D. J., Gabbett, T. J., & Nassis, G. P. (2016). Agility in team sports: Testing, training and factors affecting performance. *Sports Medicine*, 46(3), 421–442. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-015-0428-2
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Highly reliable ($\text{ICC} > 0.90$, $\text{CV} \le 2.0\%$). Crucially isolates mechanical turning capacity from cognitive reaction time.

### PROTOCOL 9: FTB-UNI-PHY-10 — DAYA TAHAN

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-10` — Daya Tahan
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Intermittent high-intensity aerobic endurance and recovery capacity.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The capacity to repeatedly perform high-intensity intermittent aerobic exercise and rapidly restore phosphocreatine and acid-base homeostasis during brief recovery intervals (Bangsbo et al., 2008).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Yo-Yo Intermittent Recovery Test Level 1 (YYIR1).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Football matches consist of repeated high-intensity efforts interspersed with brief recovery periods. YYIR1 distance correlates strongly with match running performance (high-intensity running distance $>19.8\text{ km/h}$, $r = 0.70–0.77$) and distinguishes competition standards across elite academies (Krustrup et al., 2003).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Standardized calibrated Yo-Yo IR1 audio sound track (acoustically verified beeps).
   - High-output PA sound system / Bluetooth speaker ($\ge 90\text{ dB}$).
   - Measuring tape ($30\text{m}$) and cones.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Verify audio file duration (audio calibration beep check at start of track). Pitch temperature $\le 30^\circ\text{C}$.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** A $25\text{ m}$ corridor:
   - Start/Finish Line (Cone A) to Turning Line (Cone B): exactly $20.0\text{ m}$.
   - Recovery Area (Cone C): $5.0\text{ m}$ behind Cone A.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** 10-minute warm-up consisting of light jogging and 2 practice shuttles of the Yo-Yo test up to level 11.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Lari bolak-balik 20 meter mengikuti bunyi 'BEEP'. Anda harus menyentuh garis 20 meter pada bunyi beep kedua, dan kembali ke garis awal pada bunyi beep ketiga. Setelah itu, lakukan jalan santai di area pemulihan 5 meter selama 10 detik sebelum beep berikutnya. Jika gagal mencapai garis tepat waktu, Anda diberi 1 kali peringatan. Pada kegagalan kedua, tes Anda selesai!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** Exactly **1 maximal attempt**.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Total distance covered in **meters (m)** (e.g., `1,480 m`) and final completed speed level. Estimated $\text{VO}_2\max$ ($\text{mL/kg/min}$) can be derived via:
    $$\text{VO}_2\max = (\text{YYIR1 distance in meters} \times 0.0084) + 36.4$$
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Shuttles completed on or before beep; full $20\text{ m}$ distance reached.
    - *Invalid / Termination:* Failing to reach the line twice consecutively; turning short of the line; pacing violation.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Evaluators monitor athletes (maximum 4 athletes per evaluator). Scribe marks completed shuttles on tracking grid.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Maximal cardiovascular test. Contraindicated in athletes with recent viral illness ($<7$ days), asthma flare-up, or cardiovascular anomalies. Mandatory AED and ice towels on site.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Mandatory for T3 (U14–U16) and T4 (Senior). Optional submaximal threshold in T2. **Strictly prohibited in T1 (U8–U10)**.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Extremely demanding for Central Midfielders (`CM`, `DM`, `AM`) and Wingbacks (`WB`).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Krustrup, P., Mohr, M., Amstrup, T., Rysgaard, T., Johansen, J., Steensberg, A., Pedersen, P. K., & Bangsbo, J. (2003). The Yo-Yo intermittent recovery test: Physiological response, reliability, and validity. *Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise*, 35(4), 697–705. https://doi.org/10.1249/01.MSS.0000058441.94520.32
    - Bangsbo, J., Iaia, F. M., & Krustrup, P. (2008). The Yo-Yo intermittent recovery test: A useful tool for evaluation of physical performance in football. *Sports Medicine*, 38(1), 37–51. https://doi.org/10.2165/00007256-200838010-00004
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Excellent test-retest reliability ($\text{CV} \le 4.9\%$, $\text{ICC} > 0.90$). Limitation: Highly susceptible to mental pacing strategies; grouping competitive athletes together improves performance by $5–8\%$.

### PROTOCOL 10: FTB-UNI-PHY-11 — DAYA LEDAK

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-11` — Daya Ledak
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Maximum horizontal explosive power and horizontal rate of force development (RFD).
3. **Scientific Definition:** The maximum horizontal center-of-mass propulsion generated by the hip, knee, and ankle extensors in a simultaneous bilateral horizontal jump, utilizing arms for momentum transfer (Comfort et al., 2014).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Standing Broad Jump (Standing Long Jump).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Unlike vertical jumping (which reflects vertical stiffness), horizontal jumping specifically assesses horizontal force production capacity, which correlates more strongly ($r = 0.72$) with initial $0–10\text{m}$ sprint acceleration in soccer players (Maulder & Cronin, 2005).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Standardized non-slip standing broad jump mat with integrated centimeter markings, or high-friction rubber runway with steel measuring tape.
   - Wooden takeoff line block.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Tape measure zero aligned flush with the front takeoff edge.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Minimum $4\text{ m} \times 1.5\text{ m}$ flat, slip-resistant surface.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Integrated into combine jump station after RAMP warm-up. 2 submaximal practice jumps.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di belakang garis lepas landas dengan kedua kaki sejajar. Ayunkan kedua lengan dan tekuk lutut untuk mengambil awalan, lalu melompat ke depan sejauh-jauhnya! Mendaratlah dengan kedua kaki bersamaan dan pertahankan keseimbangan tanpa melangkah atau jatuh ke belakang."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 3 valid trials. Rest interval: **60 seconds** between jumps.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Distance in **centimeters (cm)** measured to nearest $0.5\text{ cm}$ (e.g., `218.5 cm`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Two-footed takeoff without pre-hop or step; stable two-footed landing without falling backwards or hands touching floor behind feet.
    - *Invalid:* Stepping over takeoff line prior to jump; single-leg takeoff; falling backward or placing hand down behind landing mark.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Measurement is taken from the takeoff line to the point of contact of the **rear-most heel** upon landing. Best of 3 attempts recorded.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Knee and ankle impact upon landing. Ensure landing surface is shock-absorbent (e.g., dense athletic mat or turf).
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** All tiers: T1 to T4. Highly accessible, safe motor power metric.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Highly indicative of acceleration potential across all outfield positions.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Comfort, P., Stewart, A., Bloom, L., & Clarkson, B. (2014). Relationships between strength, sprint, and jump performance in well-trained youth soccer players. *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 28(1), 173–177. https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0b013e318295d267
    - Maulder, P., & Cronin, J. (2005). Horizontal and vertical jump assessment: Reliability, symmetry, and validity. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 23(1), 45–54. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640410410001730079
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Very high reliability ($\text{ICC} > 0.93$, $\text{CV} \le 2.5\%$). Valid and reliable indicator of bilateral horizontal explosive capacity.

### PROTOCOL 11: FTB-UNI-PHY-12 — KEKUATAN TUBUH

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-12` — Kekuatan Tubuh
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Maximum isometric lower/upper-body muscular strength or upper-body relative muscular endurance.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The peak force-generating capacity of skeletal muscle during a standardized multi-joint contraction without change in joint angle (Haff et al., 2015).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:**
   - *Primary (T3–T4):* Isometric Mid-Thigh Pull (IMTP) using dual axial force plates.
   - *Field Proxy (T1–T2):* Standardized 1-Minute Maximum Push-Up Test (cadence-controlled or maximum continuous repetitions to failure per ACSM standards).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Maximum strength underpins sprinting speed, change of direction, jumping height, and reduces hamstring and ACL injury risk. However, 1RM barbell testing (squat/bench) poses significant injury risks in adolescent athletes without mature lifting technique (Lloyd & Oliver, 2012). IMTP provides a zero-movement, zero-injury-risk metric of peak force.
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - IMTP: Custom rigid pull rack with dual force plates ($1,000\text{ Hz}$) and immovable barbell bar.
   - Field Proxy: Non-slip exercise mat, $10\text{ cm}$ foam chest block, digital metronome ($60\text{ bpm}$).
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Force plates calibrated with certified weights; zero-load tare before athlete steps on plates.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Solid level concrete floor for IMTP rack.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up with specific isometric hip/knee extension potentiation.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):**
    - *IMTP:* *"Posisikan tubuh seperti awalan deadlift (lutut ditekuk $125^\circ-135^\circ$, pinggul $140^\circ-150^\circ$). Pegang bar erat-erat dengan strap. Saat aba-aba '3, 2, 1, TARIK!', dorong lantai sekuat-kuatnya ke bawah selama 5 detik penuh seolah-olah ingin menembus lantai!"*
    - *Push-Up Proxy:* *"Lakukan push-up dengan tubuh lurus sempurna dari kepala hingga tumit. Turunkan dada hingga menyentuh balok 10 cm, lalu dorong kembali ke atas hingga siku lurus sempurna."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:**
    - IMTP: 2 maximal trials of 5 seconds. Rest interval: **3 minutes**.
    - Push-Up: 1 continuous attempt to volitional fatigue.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - IMTP: Peak Force in **Newtons (N)** and Relative Peak Force ($\text{N/kg}$).
    - Push-Up Proxy: Completed valid repetitions (integer count).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *IMTP Valid:* No pre-tension countermovement (slight dip before pull invalidates trial); maximum pull sustained for $\ge 3\text{ s}$.
    - *Push-Up Valid:* Full elbow extension at top; chest contacts block; hips do not sag or pike.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Direct raw export from force plate software.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Back injuries, disc herniation, or acute spinal issues contraindicate IMTP.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** IMTP strictly for T3 (U14+) and T4. Push-up proxy used for T1 and T2.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal. Center-Backs, Strikers, and Goalkeepers require higher absolute peak force.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Haff, G. G., Ruben, R. P., Lider, J., Twine, C., & Cormie, P. (2015). A systematic review of the isometric mid-thigh pull. *Journal of Strength and Conditioning Research*, 29(6), 1736–1750. https://doi.org/10.1519/JSC.0000000000000858
    - Lloyd, R. S., & Oliver, J. L. (2012). The youth physical development model: A new approach to long-term athletic development. *Strength & Conditioning Journal*, 34(3), 61–72. https://doi.org/10.1519/SSC.0b013e31825760ea
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **REQUIRES SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION (Due to Equipment Disparity)**. IMTP is gold standard ($\text{ICC} > 0.95$), but force plates are rarely available in grassroots academies. Push-up proxies measure relative upper-body muscular endurance, not lower-body football strength.

### PROTOCOL 12: FTB-UNI-PHY-13 — KEKUATAN INTI

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-13` — Kekuatan Inti
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Anterior and lateral lumbo-pelvic-hip core isometric muscular endurance.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability of the trunk musculature (rectus abdominis, transversus abdominis, obliques, erector spinae) to maintain a neutral spine against gravitational torque over prolonged isometric holding (McGill et al., 1999).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Standardized Prone Isometric Bridge (Plank Hold Test).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** In soccer, core stiffness stabilizes the pelvis during sprinting, kicking, and physical shoulder-to-shoulder duels. Core fatigue leads to compensatory lumbar hyperextension, increasing hamstring strain and groin injury risks (McGill et al., 1999).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - High-density exercise floor mat ($2\text{ cm}$ thick).
   - Digital stopwatch calibrated to $0.01\text{ s}$.
   - Wooden dowel ($1.0\text{ m}$) for spinal alignment verification.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Level floor mat; stopwatch time base verified.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat indoor floor area.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Performed after dynamic warm-up. Brief 10-second posture check.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Posisikan tubuh bertumpu pada kedua siku dan ujung jari kaki. Siku tepat di bawah bahu. Pertahankan tubuh lurus sempurna seperti papan dari kepala, punggung, hingga tumit. Jangan biarkan pinggul turun atau naik. Tahan posisi ini selama mungkin hingga tidak mampu lagi mempertahankan posisi lurus."*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** Exactly **1 maximal holding attempt**.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Time elapsed in seconds to the nearest whole second (e.g., `85 s`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Spine maintains neutral alignment throughout hold.
    - *Termination:* First warning given when pelvis drops or rises $>5\text{ cm}$; test terminated on second departure from neutral posture, or when athlete voluntary drops to knees.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Scribe starts stopwatch on lift-off and stops upon second pelvic deviation or ground touch. Record total seconds.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Low injury risk. Athletes with acute lumbar disc pathology are contraindicated.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** All tiers: T1 to T4.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - McGill, S. M., Childs, A., & Liebenson, C. (1999). Endurance times for low back stabilization exercises: Clinical targets for testing and training from a normal database. *Archives of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation*, 80(8), 941–944. https://doi.org/10.1016/S0003-9993(99)90087-4
    - Hibbs, A. E., Thompson, K. G., French, D., Wrigley, A., & Spears, I. (2008). Optimizing performance by improving core stability and core strength. *Sports Medicine*, 38(12), 995–1008. https://doi.org/10.2165/00007256-200838120-00004
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Good reliability ($\text{ICC} = 0.88–0.91$). Limitation: Measures muscular endurance rather than high-rate dynamic rotational power.

### PROTOCOL 13: FTB-UNI-PHY-14 — SPRINT BERULANG

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-14` — Sprint Berulang
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Repeated Sprint Ability (RSA), anaerobic glycolytic recovery, and sprint decrement fatigue resistance.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability to execute repeated maximal or near-maximal sprint efforts with brief recovery intervals ($<30\text{ s}$), reflecting phosphocreatine (PCr) resynthesis kinetics and muscle buffering capacity (Girard et al., 2011).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Bangsbo Repeated Sprint Ability Test: $6 \times 30\text{ m}$ linear sprints with active recovery ($20\text{ s}$ turnaround return jog) every $25\text{ s}$.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** High-intensity counter-attacks and pressing sequences require back-to-back maximum sprints. Players with poor RSA experience massive velocity drop-offs during intense match phases, compromising defensive recovery and offensive transitions (Bishop et al., 2011).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 2 pairs of dual-beam photocell timing gates set at $0.0\text{ m}$ and $30.0\text{ m}$.
   - Audio countdown timing system with automatic beeps at $15\text{ s}$, $20\text{ s}$, and $25\text{ s}$.
   - Cones marking return deceleration and recovery loop ($10\text{ m}$ past finish line).
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Gates aligned and synchronized to master interval timer.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** A $45\text{ m} \times 10\text{ m}$ pitch area (30m sprint track + 15m return loop).
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Thorough RAMP warm-up with dynamic sprint potentiation.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Anda akan melakukan 6 kali sprint maksimal sejauh 30 meter. Setiap sprint dimulai dari posisi diam setelah hitungan mundur '3, 2, 1, GO!'. Setelah melewati garis 30 meter, segera melambat, berbalik santai, dan kembali ke garis start dalam waktu 20 detik. Semua 6 sprint harus dilakukan dengan usaha MAKSIMAL 100%!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** Exactly **1 test battery** of 6 sprints. Sprints start every $25\text{ s}$ (sprint duration ~4.2s + active recovery ~20.8s).
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:**
    - Best Sprint Time ($S_{\text{best}}$, seconds, two decimals).
    - Mean Sprint Time ($S_{\text{mean}}$, seconds, two decimals).
    - Sprint Decrement Percentage ($S_{\text{dec}}\%$):
      $$S_{\text{dec}}\% = \left(\frac{\sum_{i=1}^6 S_i}{6 \times S_{\text{best}}} - 1\right) \times 100$$
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* All 6 sprints performed with full effort; athlete arrives at start box prior to countdown.
    - *Invalid:* Pacing (sprint 1 $>5\%$ slower than athlete's fresh $30\text{m}$ PR); failing to return to start in time.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Automatic recording of all 6 individual sprint splits. Software computes $S_{\text{best}}$, $S_{\text{mean}}$, and $S_{\text{dec}}\%$.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Extreme anaerobic glycolytic demand (blood lactate $>12\text{ mmol/L}$). **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN ON THE SAME DAY AS YO-YO IR1 (48h separation required)**. Contraindicated in athletes with recent hamstring strain or dehydration.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** **Strictly T4 (Performance / U17+) and late T3 (U16 post-PHV)**. Prohibited in T1 and T2 due to immature glycolytic enzyme profiles (low phosphofructokinase activity in pre-pubertal children).
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal outfield players. Crucial for Fullbacks, Wingers, and Box-to-Box Midfielders.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Girard, O., Mendez-Villanueva, A., & Bishop, D. (2011). Repeated-sprint ability—Part I: Factors contributing to fatigue. *Sports Medicine*, 41(8), 673–694. https://doi.org/10.2165/11590550-000000000-00000
    - Bishop, D., Girard, O., & Mendez-Villanueva, A. (2011). Repeated-sprint ability—Part II: Recommendations for training. *Sports Medicine*, 41(9), 741–756. https://doi.org/10.2165/11590560-000000000-00000
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Highly valid for elite match demands ($\text{ICC} = 0.86–0.92$). Limitation: Susceptible to pacing artifacts if athletes consciously conserve energy in early sprints.

### PROTOCOL 14: FTB-UNI-TEC-07 — KEKUATAN TEMBAKAN

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-UNI-TEC-07` — Kekuatan Tembakan
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Maximum ball exit velocity during a stationary instep kick.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The peak linear velocity of the football immediately following foot-ball impact during a maximal instep drive, reflecting segmental angular velocities of the kicking limb, linear foot velocity at impact, and the coefficient of restitution (Lees et al., 2010).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Stationary Ball Instep Kick Radar Test.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Ball speed directly determines goalkeeper reaction time window. Elite adult players generate ball speeds $>110\text{ km/h}$, while youth players range from $65$ to $95\text{ km/h}$. Measuring exit velocity isolates pure mechanical striking power from tactical finishing accuracy (Lees et al., 2010; Markovic & Mikulic, 2014).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Calibrated sports Doppler radar gun (e.g., Stalker Sport 2 or Bushnell Speedster III) with digital display.
   - Heavy-duty tripod mounting the radar gun at ball flight height ($0.50\text{ m}$ above ground).
   - Standard FIFA Quality Pro footballs inflated to exactly **$0.80\text{ bar}$ ($11.6\text{ psi}$)**.
   - Regulation goal ($7.32\text{ m} \times 2.44\text{ m}$) with target netting.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Radar gun tuning fork calibration prior to testing. Ball pressure verified using digital pressure gauge before every athlete.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Penalty spot or $16\text{ m}$ mark from goal. Radar gun positioned behind the goal net directly in line with the ball trajectory. Kicking box marked with cones.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Full RAMP warm-up with progressive kicking warm-up: 3 short passes, 2 half-effort shots, 1 full-effort shot per leg.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Bola diam di titik penalti. Ambil awalan bebas sesuai kenyamanan Anda (biasanya 2-4 langkah). Tendang bola sekeras-kerasnya menggunakan punggung kaki (instep drive) mengarah ke tengah gawang!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 3 valid trials with Dominant Foot, 3 valid trials with Non-Dominant Foot. Rest interval: **45–60 seconds** between kicks.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Peak velocity in **kilometers per hour (km/h)** to **one decimal place** (e.g., `88.5 km/h`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Clean instep strike; ball flies straight toward target net within a $\pm 10^\circ$ window from radar beam.
    - *Invalid:* Ball struck with toe or heel; ball misses target frame (excessive cosine angle error $>15^\circ$ falsely reduces radar reading); miskick or ground contact prior to ball.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Scribe notes radar reading immediately after impact. Highest recorded speed for dominant and non-dominant feet recorded; foot asymmetry ratio calculated.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Groin and rectus femoris strain risks. Avoid in athletes with hip flexor apophysitis (common in pre-PHV adolescents).
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Recommended from T2 (U11+) upwards. In T1, instep kicking power is evaluated qualitatively to protect immature pelvic growth plates.
17. **Position Applicability:** Universal outfield players. High relevance for Strikers (`ST`), Second Strikers (`SS`), Attacking Midfielders (`AM`), and Center-Backs (`CB` for long clearance velocity).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Lees, A., Asai, T., Andersen, T. B., Nunome, H., & Sterzing, T. (2010). The biomechanics of kicking in soccer: A review. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 28(8), 805–817. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2010.481305
    - Markovic, G., & Mikulic, P. (2014). Assessment of kicking performance in soccer. *Kinesiology*, 46(1), 3–15.
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Highly reliable ($\text{ICC} > 0.90$, $\text{CV} \le 3.2\%$) provided ball pressure and radar cosine angle are strictly standardized. Limitation: Does not measure accuracy or decision-making under defensive pressure (which remains under `FTB-UNI-TEC-05 Finishing` observation rubric).

### PROTOCOL 15: FTB-POS-WF-01 — AKSELERASI SAYAP

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-POS-WF-01` — Akselerasi Sayap
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Wing-specific curved acceleration and high-speed transition along the flank corridor.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability to execute a high-speed curved sprint trajectory along the touchline corridor, transitioning from an angled body orientation to linear vertical speed while resisting centrifugal acceleration forces (Fílter et al., 2020).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** $30\text{m}$ Curved Sprint Test along a standardized $17\text{m}$ radius arc (representing typical winger overlapping and diagonal underlapping sprint paths).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Modern wingers and wingbacks rarely sprint in straight lines; $>80\%$ of high-speed runs on the flank are curved around defenders. Curved sprinting imposes asymmetric ground reaction forces and higher inner/outer limb lateral cutting demands than straight sprinting (Fílter et al., 2020).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 3 pairs of dual-beam photocell timing gates ($0.0\text{ m}$, $15.0\text{ m}$ arc midpoint, $30.0\text{ m}$ exit).
   - Curved track layout cones calibrated to a $17.0\text{ m}$ radius circle arc.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Gate height set to $0.85\text{ m}$. Laser aligned along the chord of entry and exit.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** A $35\text{ m} \times 15\text{ m}$ area on the flank of a dry pitch.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** Specific curved running drills (figure-8 build-ups) after RAMP warm-up.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Mulai dari posisi diam di garis start. Lari sprint secepat mungkin mengikuti lengkungan lintasan sayap melewati gerbang 15 meter dan terus memacu kecepatan menembus gerbang keluar 30 meter tanpa keluar dari koridor kerucut!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 trials curving to the Left (simulating Left Wing track), 2 trials curving to the Right (simulating Right Wing track). Rest interval: **3 minutes** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Total $30\text{m}$ curved time and split times in seconds to **two decimal places** (e.g., `4.18 s`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Sprint executed within corridor; no cone knockdown; smooth lean without stumble.
    - *Invalid:* Stepping across inner boundary; slipping or touching ground with hands.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Record best time for left-curving and right-curving sprints. Calculate directional asymmetry.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** High ankle eversion and lateral knee shear stress. Pitch must be dry.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** Position-specific test for T3 (U14–U16) and T4 (Performance).
17. **Position Applicability:** Specialized for Winger / Sayap (`WFR/WFL`), Wingback (`WB`), and Outside Midfielders (`LM/RM`).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Fílter, A., Olivares, J., Reyes-Ferrer, A., Robles-Palacio, R., Yáñez-García, J. M., & Dos'Santos, T. (2020). Curved sprinting in soccer: Relationship with linear sprint and change of direction speed. *Journal of Sports Sciences*, 38(11-12), 1320–1330. https://doi.org/10.1080/02640414.2020.1743152
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. Demonstrates high ecological validity for wide players. Reliable when track radius is precisely marked ($\text{ICC} > 0.88$).

### PROTOCOL 16: FTB-SSB-OUT-02 — KOORDINASI MATA-KAKI

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-SSB-OUT-02` — Koordinasi Mata-Kaki
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Visuomotor lower-limb coordination, rhythmic footwork, and rapid ground ball rebound control in young foundation athletes.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The neuromuscular ability to visually track a moving ball and execute accurate, alternating lower-extremity strikes and controls against a rebounding surface within a constrained temporal window (Vandorpe et al., 2012; Faber et al., 2014).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** 30-Second Alternate-Foot Rebound Wall Pass-and-Control Test.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** In foundation grassroots football (SSB / T1: U8–U10), static isolated technical drills fail to measure dynamic ball perception. This standardized rebound test captures the essential perceptual-motor synergy required for modern passing and receiving (Ali, 2011).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Solid vertical rebound wall (or standardized heavy wooden rebound board $2.0\text{ m} \times 0.5\text{ m}$).
   - Size 4 football inflated to $0.70\text{ bar}$.
   - Cones marking a $2.5\text{ m} \times 2.5\text{ m}$ player containment box $2.0\text{ m}$ from the wall.
   - Digital stopwatch.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Wall rebound coefficient verified (ball dropped from 2m must bounce $\ge 1.0\text{ m}$).
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Smooth indoor wall or solid outdoor arena perimeter wall.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** 5 practice passes against the wall with each foot.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di dalam kotak penanda. Saat peluit berbunyi 'MULAI!', oper bola ke dinding menggunakan kaki kanan, kontrol dan oper kembali menggunakan kaki kiri secara bergantian. Lakukan operan bersih sebanyak-banyaknya selama 30 detik. Bola harus tetap berada di dalam area kotak!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 trials of 30 seconds. Rest interval: **90 seconds** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Total number of successful alternating passes completed in 30 seconds (integer count, e.g., `22 pass`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Ball hits wall from within box; alternating foot sequence maintained (Right-Left-Right-Left); ball handled without hands.
    - *Invalid:* Striking twice consecutively with the same foot (does not count as an alternating score); handball; stepping completely out of box.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Evaluator counts aloud each valid alternating pass. Scribe records total for trial 1 and trial 2; highest count is retained.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Low physical injury risk. Ensure clear space behind player.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** **Strictly designed for Tier 1 Foundation (SSB / U8–U10)**.
17. **Position Applicability:** Outfield SSB players (`OUTFIELD_SSB`).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Ali, A. (2011). Measuring soccer skill performance: A review. *Scandinavian Journal of Medicine & Science in Sports*, 21(2), 170–183. https://doi.org/10.1111/j.1600-0838.2010.01256.x
    - Vandorpe, B., Vandendriessche, J. B., Vaeyens, R., Pion, J., Lefevre, J., Philippaerts, R. M., & Lenoir, M. (2012). The KörperkoordinationsTest für Kinder: Reference values and suitability for identifying talented young athletes. *Pediatric Exercise Science*, 24(2), 215–228. https://doi.org/10.1123/pes.24.2.215
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality for Youth)**. Highly engaging and reliable test of foundational coordination in children ($\text{ICC} = 0.85$). Does not predict adult elite performance, but accurately reflects neuromuscular developmental stage.

### PROTOCOL 17: FTB-SSB-GK-02 — KOORDINASI TANGAN-MATA

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-SSB-GK-02` — Koordinasi Tangan-Mata
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Visuomotor upper-limb manual dexterity, hand-eye coordination, and visual tracking speed for introductory goalkeepers.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The neuromuscular coordination of visual inputs with fine and gross motor movements of the hands and arms to intercept and securely capture an incoming projectile (Faber et al., 2014).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Alternate-Hand Wall Toss and Catch Test (30 Seconds).
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Foundation goalkeepers (T1: U8–U10) must develop basic hand-eye tracking and manual dexterity before learning advanced diving or handling biomechanics. The wall toss test is the international standard for assessing hand-eye motor competence in pediatric sports science (Faber et al., 2014).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - Standard tennis ball (standard pressure and bounce).
   - Solid vertical brick or concrete wall.
   - Measuring tape.
   - Masking tape marking athlete standing line at exactly $2.0\text{ m}$ from wall.
   - Digital stopwatch.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Ball bounce verified; wall distance verified to $\pm 1\text{ cm}$.
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat indoor/outdoor area with smooth vertical wall.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** 5 practice throws and catches.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di belakang garis 2 meter. Lempar bola ke dinding dengan tangan kanan (underhand / lemparan dari bawah) dan tangkap dengan tangan kiri. Lalu lempar dengan tangan kiri dan tangkap dengan tangan kanan. Lakukan sebanyak-banyaknya selama 30 detik tanpa menjatuhkan bola!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 trials of 30 seconds. Rest interval: **60 seconds** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Number of successful alternate catches completed in 30 seconds (integer count, e.g., `24 catches`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* Ball thrown underhand; caught cleanly with alternate hand before touching ground; feet remain behind $2\text{ m}$ line.
    - *Invalid:* Overhand throwing; catching with two hands; catching with the same hand that threw; stepping over the line. If ball drops, timer continues while athlete retrieves it.
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Scribe counts valid catches. Best score of 2 trials is recorded.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Low injury risk.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** **Strictly designed for Tier 1 SSB Goalkeepers (`GK_SSB`)**.
17. **Position Applicability:** Introductory Goalkeepers.
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Faber, I. R., Bustin, P. M., Oosterveld, F. G., Elferink-Gemser, M. T., & Nijhuis-Van der Sanden, M. W. (2014). Assessing personal talent determinants in young table tennis players: A systematic review. *Sports Medicine*, 44(3), 391–406. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-013-0124-7
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality for Pediatric Motor Assessment)**. Well-established reliability in pediatric motor batteries ($\text{ICC} = 0.84–0.89$).

### PROTOCOL 18: FTB-SSB-GK-03 — KETANGKASAN GERAK DASAR

1. **Canonical ID & Name:** `FTB-SSB-GK-03` — Ketangkasan Gerak Dasar
2. **Physical/Technical Attribute Measured:** Fundamental multi-directional footwork agility, dynamic balance, and stepping coordination.
3. **Scientific Definition:** The ability to execute rapid, multi-directional bilateral hopping and stepping transitions around a standardized geometric obstacle while maintaining dynamic postural stability (Beas-Jiménez et al., 2011).
4. **Recommended Test Protocol:** Standardized Hexagon Agility Test.
5. **Why This Test is Appropriate:** Goalkeeping footwork requires lightning-fast, multi-planar adjustments around the goal mouth without crossing feet. The Hexagon test specifically assesses high-frequency multi-directional stepping without requiring massive running momentum, making it ideal for young goalkeepers (T1: U8–U10) (Beas-Jiménez et al., 2011).
6. **Equipment Required:**
   - 6 strips of high-visibility floor tape or PVC hexagon grid ($60.5\text{ cm}$ side length, $120^\circ$ internal angles).
   - Digital stopwatch calibrated to $0.01\text{ s}$.
7. **Equipment Calibration:** Hexagon dimensions verified: side length exactly $60.5\text{ cm}$ ($24\text{ inches}$).
8. **Testing Area & Setup Dimensions:** Flat, non-slip indoor floor or firm artificial turf.
9. **Athlete Preparation & Warm-up:** 1 practice circuit (3 hops) to learn rotation direction.
10. **Exact Testing Instructions (Indonesian):** *"Berdiri di tengah segi enam menghadap ke depan. Saat aba-aba 'MULAI!', lompat dengan kedua kaki bersamaan keluar melewati salah satu sisi, lalu segera lompat kembali ke tengah. Lanjutkan ke sisi berikutnya searah jarum jam sampai menyelesaikan 3 putaran penuh secepat mungkin tanpa menyentuh garis!"*
11. **Number of Attempts & Rest Intervals:** 2 trials (1 clockwise, 1 counter-clockwise). Rest interval: **2 minutes** between trials.
12. **Raw Measurement Format & Units:** Total time to complete 3 full circuits in seconds to **two decimal places** (e.g., `12.45 s`).
13. **Valid and Invalid Attempts:**
    - *Valid:* 3 complete 6-sided circuits completed; double-leg takeoff and landing maintained throughout.
    - *Penalty / Invalid:* Stepping on or landing on top of tape lines adds a $0.5\text{ s}$ penalty per infraction. Complete loss of balance or turning body orientation invalidates trial (re-test allowed).
14. **Data Recording Procedure:** Scribe records raw time and number of line-touch penalties. Final score = Raw Time + (Penalties $\times 0.5\text{s}$). Best of clockwise/counter-clockwise recorded.
15. **Safety Precautions & Contraindications:** Ankle inversion risk. Surface must be clean and slip-resistant.
16. **Age/Development Applicability:** **Tier 1 Foundation (SSB / U8–U10) Goalkeepers**.
17. **Position Applicability:** Foundation Goalkeepers (`GK_SSB`).
18. **Scientific References:**
    - Beas-Jiménez, J. D., López-Valverde, M. A., & Morales-Sánchez, F. J. (2011). Evaluation of agility in sports: A systematic review. *Revista Andaluza de Medicina del Deporte*, 4(1), 26–34.
    - Paul, D. J., Gabbett, T. J., & Nassis, G. P. (2016). Agility in team sports: Testing, training and factors affecting performance. *Sports Medicine*, 46(3), 421–442. https://doi.org/10.1007/s40279-015-0428-2
19. **Evidence Quality & Limitations:** **PASS (High Quality)**. High reliability in youth sports ($\text{ICC} = 0.88–0.92$). Isolates rapid short-amplitude stepping from linear sprinting.
