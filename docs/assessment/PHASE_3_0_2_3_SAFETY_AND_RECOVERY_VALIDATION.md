### 1. SCIENTIFIC RE-EVALUATION OF THE 23-MINUTE PRE-YO-YO RECOVERY WINDOW

Previous combine planning loosely asserted that the 23-minute recovery break prior to Yo-Yo IR1 achieved "glycogen and phosphocreatine replenishment." The operations audit formally corrects this claim based on established exercise biochemistry:

```
====================================================================================================
BIOENERGETIC RECOVERY STATE AFTER 23 MINUTES OF PASSIVE REST + NUTRITIONAL INTAKE
====================================================================================================
Physiological Substrate      | Biochemical Status at Minute 23       | Evidence & References
-----------------------------|----------------------------------------|-----------------------------
Phosphocreatine (PCr) Stores | **100% Fully Resynthesized**           | Harris et al. (1976);
                             | (PCr t1/2 = 60–90s; >95% at 5 min)     | Bogdanis et al. (1995)
Intramuscular ATP            | **100% Fully Restored**                | Sahlin et al. (1979)
Blood / Muscle Lactate       | **Significantly Cleared (~70–80%)**    | Bangsbo et al. (1993);
                             | (Half-life = 15–20 min with walking)   | Gladden (2004)
Core Body Temperature (Tc)   | **Normalized toward Baseline**         | Sawka et al. (2007)
Resting Heart Rate (HR)      | **Decreased to <100 bpm**              | ACSM (2007)
Muscle Glycogen Stores       | **PARTIALLY DEPLETED (NOT RESTORED!)** | Costill et al. (1981);
                             | (Full glycogen repletion takes 24–48h) | Ivy (1991)
====================================================================================================
```

#### Biochemical Reality & Operational Truth:
- **What 23 Minutes Does:** Restores phosphocreatine, eliminates acute neuromuscular fatigue, clears the majority of lactate accumulation from previous sprint and COD trials, restores neuromuscular motor unit firing frequency, and stabilizes blood glucose via electrolyte/carbohydrate beverage ingestion ($500\text{ ml}$ of $6\%$ carbohydrate solution).
- **What 23 Minutes Cannot Do:** It **CANNOT** resynthesize depleted intramuscular glycogen. Athletes will enter Yo-Yo IR1 with approximately $75\%–85\%$ of normal resting muscle glycogen. Because Yo-Yo IR1 is an intermittent exercise relying heavily on muscle glycogen, athletes will reach terminal exhaustion primarily through peripheral glycogen depletion and severe metabolic acidosis ($\text{pH} < 7.1$). This is the intended physiological end-point of the test, provided neuromuscular alactic freshness was restored.

### 2. TIER-SPECIFIC YO-YO IR1 POLICY & STOPPING CRITERIA

A critical deficiency in amateur scouting is prescribing identical exhaustion batteries to all athletes regardless of developmental stage. CHUVATOR enforces strict tier differentiation:

```
====================================================================================================
DEVELOPMENTAL ELIGIBILITY MATRIX: YO-YO INTERMITTENT RECOVERY LEVEL 1 (YYIR1)
====================================================================================================
Curriculum Tier  | Age Target | Biological Maturity Target | Yo-Yo IR1 Policy & Protocol Standard
-----------------|------------|----------------------------|----------------------------------------
T1: Foundation   | U8 – U10   | Pre-PHV (Offset < -2.0 yr) | **STRICTLY PROHIBITED (BANNED)**
T2: Development  | U11 – U13  | Pre-PHV (Offset < -1.0 yr) | **SUBMAXIMAL / SYMPTOM-LIMITED ONLY**
T3: Advanced Dev | U14 – U16  | Circa/Post-PHV (Offset >0) | **MAXIMAL TEST (With Medical Clearance)**
T4: Performance  | U17+       | Post-PHV (Adult profile)   | **MAXIMAL COMBINE STANDARD**
====================================================================================================
```

#### 2.1 Symptom-Based Stopping Criteria for Tier 2 (U11–U13):
Tier 2 athletes are assessed under a symptom-limited protocol. The evaluator or line judge must terminate an athlete's test immediately upon encountering ANY of the following:
1. **Heart Rate Cap:** Heart rate reaches $\ge 90\%$ of age-predicted maximum ($\text{HR}_{\max} = 208 - 0.7 \times \text{Age}$).
2. **Perceived Exertion Cap:** Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE on Borg 6–20 scale) reaches $\ge 17$ ("Very Hard").
3. **Biomechanical Breakdown:** Severe upper trunk sway, stumbling, or loss of head control during deceleration.
4. **Distance Cap:** The test is halted automatically upon reaching **$800\text{ meters}$ (Level 14.5)**. The athlete is credited with Level 14.5; the record is flagged as `SUBMAXIMAL_CAPPED`.

#### 2.2 Medical Exclusions for All Tiers:
- Recent viral illness or fever within prior 7 days.
- Known exercise-induced bronchospasm (asthma) without personal inhaler on pitch.
- History of syncope, dizziness, or chest pain during exercise.

### 3. WET-BULB GLOBE TEMPERATURE (WBGT) HEAT SAFETY PROTOCOL

Grounding: FIFA Medical Network Environmental Heat Policy & American College of Sports Medicine (ACSM, 2007) Position Stand: *Exertional Heat Illness during Training and Competition*.

```
====================================================================================================
WBGT HEAT ACTION PROTOCOL & THRESHOLDS (MONITORED EVERY 30 MINUTES)
====================================================================================================
WBGT Index Range             | Risk Level  | Operational Mandate & Combine Modifications
-----------------------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------
< 28.0°C (< 82.4°F)          | **GREEN**   | Standard 210-minute combine operations proceed.
                             | (Low Risk)  | Standard hydration available at all stations.
-----------------------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------
28.0°C – 30.0°C (82.4–86.0°F)| **AMBER**   | Mandatory 2-minute shaded hydration breaks injected
                             | (Moderate)  | at the 11-minute mark of every station rotation.
                             |             | Staff 02 prepares cold wet sponges at station perimeter.
-----------------------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------
30.1°C – 32.2°C (86.1–89.9°F)| **RED**     | Station circuit rotation times reduced to 18 minutes;
                             | (High Risk) | **Yo-Yo IR1 is CANCELLED / POSTPONED** to the following
                             |             | morning at 06:30 WIB.
-----------------------------|-------------|--------------------------------------------------------
> 32.2°C (> 89.9°F)          | **BLACK**   | **IMMEDIATE COMBINE TERMINATION / ABORT**.
                             | (Extreme)   | All athletes relocated to air-conditioned shelter.
====================================================================================================
```

#### Monitoring Protocol:
- **Apparatus:** Kestrel 5400 Heat Stress Meter on a $1.2\text{m}$ tripod at pitch center.
- **Schedule:** Logged at 07:15 (pre-test baseline), 07:45, 08:15, 08:45, 09:15, 09:45, 10:15, 10:45 WIB by Staff 01.

### 4. YO-YO IR1 HEAT LOGISTICS & DROPOUT SAFETY

#### 4.1 Running Corridor Dimensions & Lane Allocation
- Pitch width partitioned into **10 parallel running lanes**:
  - Lane width: exactly $2.0\text{ m}$ per athlete (Total track width: $20.0\text{ m}$).
  - Start line (Cone A) to Turning line (Cone B): exactly $20.0\text{ m}$.
  - Recovery zone (Cone C): $5.0\text{ m}$ behind Cone A.

#### 4.2 Minute-by-Minute Heat Timeline:
- **Heat 1 (Squads A & B, 10 Athletes):**
  - `10:15 – 10:17 (2 min)`: Pitch call, line assignments, PA calibration beep test.
  - `10:17 – 10:28 (11 min)`: Test execution. (Dropouts typically occur between minute 4 / Level 12 and minute 11 / Level 17.5).
  - `10:28 – 10:30 (2 min)`: Track exit, cold sponge distribution, and score logging.
- **Heat 2 (Squads C & D, 10 Athletes):**
  - `10:30 – 10:32 (2 min)`: Positioning and briefing.
  - `10:32 – 10:43 (11 min)`: Test execution.
  - `10:43 – 10:45 (2 min)`: Track exit and final score confirmation.
- **Total Duration:** Exactly **30.0 minutes**.

#### 4.3 Safe Dropout Management:
1. When an athlete fails to reach the line twice consecutively, Line Judge 1 signals failure and calls the bib number.
2. The field scribe logs the last completed shuttle.
3. The athlete **steps laterally off the active track** into the shaded sideline recovery lane.
4. Staff 02 (Physiotherapist) performs an immediate visual screen, hands the athlete cold hydration, and monitors walking cool-down. At no point is an exhausted athlete allowed to collapse on the active running track.
