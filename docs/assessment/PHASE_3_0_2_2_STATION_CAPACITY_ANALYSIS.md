#### STATION 2: EXPLOSIVE POWER (CMJ & STANDING BROAD JUMP)
- **Cohort:** 5 athletes.
- **Workflow Model:** Interleaved Serial Jumps (Maximizing Neuromuscular Rest).
- **Time Analysis:**
  - *Test 1: Countermovement Jump (CMJ):*
    - Athlete A steps onto Optojump mat, performs Jump 1, steps off. Duration: $15\text{ s}$.
    - Athlete B performs Jump 1 ($15\text{ s}$), followed by C, D, E.
    - 1 full rotation of 5 athletes takes $5 \times 15\text{ s} = 75\text{ s} (1.25\text{ min})$.
    - Each athlete naturally receives **$60\text{ s}$ of true passive rest** while teammates jump!
    - 3 complete CMJ rotations for all 5 athletes $= 3 \times 1.25\text{ min} = \mathbf{3.75\ minutes}$.
  - *Inter-Test Transition & Instruction:* **2.0 minutes**.
  - *Test 2: Standing Broad Jump:*
    - 5 athletes perform 3 maximal trials in identical interleaved rotation.
    - Duration per jump + tape read: $30\text{ s}$.
    - 5 athletes $\times 3$ trials $\times 30\text{ s} = \mathbf{7.5\ minutes}$.
    - Athletes receive $>2.0\text{ minutes}$ rest between maximal horizontal leaps.
  - *Data Reconciliation & Buffer:* **4.75 minutes**.
  - Total Station 2 elapsed time: $3.75 + 2.0 + 7.5 + 4.75 = \mathbf{18.0\ minutes} < 22.0\text{ minutes}$ **[PASS]**.
