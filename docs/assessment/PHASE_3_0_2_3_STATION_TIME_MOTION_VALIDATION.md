- **Personnel:** Staff 03 (Evaluator), Staff 04 (Field Scribe).
- **Physical Layout:** Two parallel testing mats separated by $3.0\text{ m}$.
  - Mat 1A: Vertical wall with millimeter tape and digital inclinometer (WBLT).
  - Mat 1B: Move2Perform Y-Balance platform (Anterior, Posteromedial, Posterolateral).
- **Timeline Breakdown:**
  - `00:00 – 01:00 (1.0 min)`: Staff 03 checks digital clinometer zero calibration against vertical plumb line; Staff 04 verifies YBT slider smoothness.
  - `01:00 – 02:30 (1.5 min)`: Squad briefing: Staff 03 demonstrates hands-on-hips YBT reach rule and non-compensatory heel contact rule for WBLT.
  - `02:30 – 17:00 (14.5 min)`: Active Testing (Concurrent Split Execution):
    - *Pair 1 (Athletes 1 & 2):* Start at Mat 1A (WBLT). 3 trials $\times$ 2 limbs $= 6$ measurements per athlete. Staff 03 records tibial angle in degrees. Elapsed: $2.5\text{ min/athlete} = 5.0\text{ min}$.
    - *Pair 2 (Athletes 3 & 4):* Start at Mat 1B (YBT). 3 practice reaches $+ 3$ recorded reaches $\times 3$ directions $\times 2$ limbs. Staff 04 records reach distance to $0.5\text{ cm}$. Elapsed: $3.5\text{ min/athlete} = 7.0\text{ min}$.
    - *At Minute 09:30:* Athletes swap mats. Pair 1 moves to YBT; Pair 2 moves to WBLT; Athlete 5 cycles through.
  - `17:00 – 18:30 (1.5 min)`: Re-test Reserve: 1 invalid reach allowed if athlete lifted heel or kicked slider plate.
  - `18:30 – 20:00 (1.5 min)`: Dual-entry audit: Staff 03 and Staff 04 cross-compare paper log vs tablet entries; compute bilateral asymmetry.
  - `20:00 – 21:00 (1.0 min)`: Mat sanitization and slide wipe-down with antiseptic towels.
  - **Total Station 1 Time: 21.0 minutes.** Safety buffer remaining: **+1.0 minute**. **[PASS]**
