### 3. CONTINGENCY BUFFERS & ENVIRONMENTAL ABORT TRIGGERS

1. **Weather Delay Buffer:** If rain or lightning occurs during the circuit, the combine halts immediately. An emergency $30\text{-minute}$ indoor hall buffer can be invoked without exceeding a $12:00\text{ WIB}$ cutoff.
2. **Wet-Bulb Globe Temperature (WBGT) Triggers:**
   - $\text{WBGT} < 28.0^\circ\text{C}$ (Normal): Standard schedule operates.
   - $28.0^\circ\text{C} \le \text{WBGT} \le 30.0^\circ\text{C}$ (Moderate): Mandatory $2\text{-minute}$ fluid break injected mid-rotation; rehydration buffer extended to $30\text{ minutes}$.
   - $30.1^\circ\text{C} \le \text{WBGT} \le 32.2^\circ\text{C}$ (High): Station rotations capped at 18 minutes; Yo-Yo IR1 postponed to next morning at 06:30 WIB.
   - $\text{WBGT} > 32.2^\circ\text{C}$ (Extreme): **ABSOLUTE COMBINE ABORT**.
