### 2. DETAILED RESOLUTION AUDIT BY GAP

#### GAP-01: Roster Shortage & Medical Tent Abandonment
- **Original Deficiency:** Phase 3.0.2.2 deployed 8 staff members, assigning 2 to each of the 4 stations. This left zero personnel at the Medical Pavilion and no roving supervisor for environmental safety.
- **Audit Stress Test:** If an athlete collapsed at Station 3, an evaluator had to abandon their station, terminating testing for their squad and halting the combine.
- **Resolution:** Established the **10-Staff Certified Model** (`PHASE_3_0_2_3_STAFF_DEPLOYMENT_MATRIX.md`). Staff 01 (Lead Sports Scientist) acts as roving marshal; Staff 02 (Physiotherapist) remains in sole command of the Medical Pavilion.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-02: Intake Station Throughput Bottleneck
- **Original Deficiency:** 20 athletes scheduled through intake screening in 25 minutes ($1.25\text{ min/athlete}$). Medical history, resting blood pressure, resting heart rate, height, sitting height, and body mass take $3.0\text{ minutes}$ minimum per athlete.
- **Resolution:** Partitioned intake into **Desk A (Physiotherapist: PAR-Q+ and vitals)** and **Desk B (Lead Sports Scientist: Seca stadiometer and digital scale)** operating in parallel. 20 athletes complete both desks in $22.5\text{ minutes}$.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-03: Station 4 Sequential Workflow Overflow
- **Original Deficiency:** Station 4 bundled 20 runs of 505 COD Test and 30 strikes of Radar Ball Exit Velocity sequentially under 1 evaluator and 1 scribe, requiring $24.2\text{ minutes}$ against a $22.0\text{-minute}$ allocation.
- **Resolution:** Mandated concurrent dual-lane operation. Staff 09 manages Lane 4A (505 photogates); Staff 10 operates Lane 4B (Stalker radar on tripod into adjacent netting). Completed in $21.0\text{ minutes}$.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-04: False Assertion of Glycogen Resynthesis
- **Original Deficiency:** Documentation asserted that the 23-minute break between circuit testing and Yo-Yo IR1 achieved "glycogen and phosphocreatine resynthesis."
- **Audit Correction:** Peer-reviewed exercise biochemistry confirms that while phosphocreatine resynthesizes in 3–5 minutes, full muscle glycogen repletion requires **24 to 48 hours**. 23 minutes only stabilizes blood glucose and clears blood lactate.
- **Resolution:** Corrected all physiological claims in `PHASE_3_0_2_3_SAFETY_AND_RECOVERY_VALIDATION.md` to reflect accurate bioenergetics.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-05: Exhaustion Testing in Immature Youth Cohorts
- **Original Deficiency:** Suggesting that Tier 1 (U8–U10) and Tier 2 (U11–U13) players perform maximal Yo-Yo IR1 exhaustion tests.
- **Resolution:** Formally banned Yo-Yo IR1 for Tier 1. Enforced a **symptom-limited protocol with an 800m ceiling cap** for Tier 2. Full maximal testing restricted to Tier 3/4 with signed medical clearance.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-06: Environmental Heat Index Verification
- **Original Deficiency:** Heat policy was loosely defined without specific instrumentation or abort criteria.
- **Resolution:** Formally integrated FIFA Medical Network and ACSM (2007) guidelines with calibrated Kestrel 5400 WBGT tracking every 30 minutes, establishing mandatory hydration pauses at $28.0^\circ\text{C}$ and absolute combine abort at $>32.2^\circ\text{C}$.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

#### GAP-07: Construct Distortion on `FTB-UNI-PHY-12`
- **Original Deficiency:** Substituting push-ups for IMTP force-plate testing.
- **Resolution:** Banned push-up proxies. Formally categorized `FTB-UNI-PHY-12` as **UNTESTED (`null`)** for the pilot combine until proper force plates are procured.
- **Status:** **REQUIRES SCIENTIFIC VALIDATION (Safely Isolated)**.

#### GAP-08: Arithmetic Schedule Inconsistency
- **Original Deficiency:** Previous reports stated 165 minutes while component sums totaled 187/198 minutes.
- **Resolution:** Authoritatively reconciled the timetable to **210 minutes (3.5 hours)** with explicit minute-by-minute accounting and zero unaccounted minutes.
- **Status:** **PASS (Resolved)**.

### 3. JUSTIFICATION FOR VERDICT: B. READY WITH CONDITIONS

The operations audit concludes that the combine documentation is **READY WITH CONDITIONS (STATUS B)** rather than an unreserved "READY":
1. **Condition 1:** The academy must formally contract and deploy **10 certified personnel** on the morning of testing. The combine **MUST NOT** proceed with only 8 staff.
2. **Condition 2:** The combine must strictly execute the **210-minute master timetable (07:30 – 11:00 WIB)**. Shortening rotations or truncating the 23-minute recovery break is prohibited.
3. **Condition 3:** `FTB-UNI-PHY-12` must remain recorded as `null` (Untested). No coach may input push-up or wall-squat scores under this canonical ID.
4. **Condition 4:** If pitch WBGT exceeds $30.0^\circ\text{C}$ at 09:45 WIB, Yo-Yo IR1 must be postponed to the following morning.
